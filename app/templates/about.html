Pipeline & Algorithms

Learn how the machine learning pipeline parses, cleans, processes, and evaluates articles to detect indicators of deception.

The Detection Pipeline

1. Natural Language Preprocessing

Raw text contains grammatical fillers, punctuation, and varying word conjugations that dilute classification signals. Our preprocessing module does the following:

  • Lowercasing: Normalizes all text to lowercase characters.
  • Punctuation & HTML Removal: Strips out URLs, special marks, and markup tags.
  • Stopword Filtering: Removes standard English high-frequency particles (e.g., "the", "and", "is") utilizing NLTK.
  • Lemmatization: Reduces words to their base lexical roots (e.g., "running", "ran" -> "run") using WordNet.

2. TF-IDF Feature Extraction

Words are mapped to numerical vectors using Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF), evaluating word relevance across the entire dataset. It highlights words unique to fake or true sources while downweighting words ubiquitous in both.

3. Logistic Regression Classifier

The system uses a calibrated Logistic Regression model, chosen for its excellent generalization ability, resistance to overfitting on text matrices, and fast computation. It fits classification margins and calculates probability scores for prediction confidence.
